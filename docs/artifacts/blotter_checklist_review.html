Three reports that were not what they looked like

Real bug

Theme choice lost on reload

The running stack serves localStorage.getItem(undefined) in its pre-paint script, so a saved theme is never read back and the OS preference always wins. Certain

Server lock holds

Lockout after five failures

Redis keeps the lock for 15 minutes by default and the server checks it before the password. A reload forgets only the countdown; the next attempt is refused with a 429 and the countdown returns. Certain in code, not exercised live

Works, rarely seen

No up or down flash

The flash rules pass their nine unit tests, but the simulated desk amends a random trade out of about 5,200, so an amend lands on a visible row roughly once every 80 minutes. Likely

The four tiles under the top bar, with the figures from the owner's screenshot. All four read every trader's active trades and ignore the blotter's filters.

The desk holds $121M more US stock than it has sold and is £1.5M short in London, at current prices. Dollars and pounds stay apart because adding them needs an exchange rate the app does not have.

unrealised + realised

Profit already banked, plus the paper result on what is still held. It covers all five seeded days rather than today, the small line is dollars only, and cancelling a trade takes its P&L back out.

Active trades 5,236 8 US names, 4 London names

count of ACTIVE trades, every trader

A JSMITH filter chip does not change this number. The names line counts symbols with at least one open trade, so a symbol with none drops out.

Feed LIVE last broadcast seq 854

socket state + number of the last broadcast

LIVE with a climbing number means the stream is flowing; LIVE with a frozen number means it is stuck. Price ticks are not numbered, and the count restarts at 1 when the server does.

The money figures move because marks tick every 900ms and the browser recalculates on each tick, with no request to the API. Certain

Why Counterparty starts empty on a required field

Every trade is a contract with someone, so the field is required. It starts empty on purpose: there is no safe default, and a prefilled "Goldman Sachs" would let a trader book against the wrong bank by pressing Enter. What was missing is on the form's side. The field was not marked required, and an empty submit showed validation jargon. It now shows as required, says "Enter a counterparty", and suggests the known banks while still accepting a new name. Certain

Amend, or cancel and rebook

The owner amended book, price, quantity and counterparty on TRD-104326, and the app recorded version 2 with an audit line, as built. The rule only forced a rebook for symbol, side and time. Counterparty now joins them. A different counterparty is a different legal contract with its own confirmation, so desks cancel and rebook rather than amend. Likely The repository had already contradicted itself here: the seed generator called a counterparty change a rebooking while the schema allowed it. No source for TP ICAP's own policy was available. Why the flash looked broken

The arrows that move all the time compare each 900ms mark with the execution price. They never flashed and still do not. The flash belongs to amendments, and the arithmetic below is why nobody saw one.

27.5sbetween simulated amends: a 5.5s average gap, 20% of actions
0.57%of amends land on the first screen: 30 rows out of 5,236 active trades
≈ 80 minbetween amends a watcher could see, before this change
≈ 55sbetween first-screen amends once half are drawn from the newest 30

The amend tint now lasts 300ms on the changed cell, the value in the motion spec, while the ▲ or ▼ beside it holds for 1.2s. With reduced motion switched on, the cell holds a still tint for 800ms instead of showing nothing.
