Anticipation & perceived performance
The product should always look like it is already ahead of the trader. Nothing here is a spinner over a blank page: every wait has a shape the eye can settle on, and every optimistic action is honest about what has and has not reached the server.

1 · SKELETON BLOTTER
Rows match the real grid's column widths and 30px height exactly, so when data lands nothing moves. Shimmer sweeps left to right in brand cyan at low opacity.
Last two rows fade out rather than ending abruptly, so the list reads as longer than the viewport. Skeleton to content is a 180ms crossfade, never a swap.
