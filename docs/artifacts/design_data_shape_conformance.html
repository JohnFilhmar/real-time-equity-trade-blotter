Which shape counts as "required"

The brief states the trade model twice and the two statements disagree. It gives a minimum interface Trade keyed on id and tradeDate with no book or counterparty, then gives sample JSON, described as "the type of data consumed by the trade blotter", keyed on tradeId and tradeTimestamp and carrying both. The "suggested fields to randomize" list matches the JSON, not the interface.

D1 is the one that needs a decision

The schema does not merely lack an AMENDED status. It rejects one, in a comment written on purpose: 

/**
 * The two states a trade can hold.
 *
 * Amending a trade is deliberately not a status: it increments `version` and writes an audit row,
 * which keeps the state machine to the two values the brief specifies.
 */
export const trade_status_values = ['ACTIVE', 'CANCELLED'] as const;
shared/src/schemas/trade.ts:7–12, and enum TradeStatus in backend/prisma/schema.prisma

The design renders AMENDED in the STATUS cell of the grid row, immediately after counterparty and time, in every direction and on both mobile list and detail. So one of the two has to move. There is also a modelling argument against the design's reading: an amended trade is still live, so AMENDED and ACTIVE are not mutually exclusive states, and collapsing them into one column loses the fact that an amended trade is tradeable.

Recommendation: the design moves. Keep the two-value enum, which is what the brief specifies and what the database already enforces, and render the amendment as a separate affordance in the same cell: an ACTIVE badge with a small amber v2 version pill beside it. That preserves the amber the brief asked for, keeps the audit trail as the place where amendments live, and costs one span in the markup. The alternative, widening the enum to three values, means a Prisma migration, a change to trade_query_schema's status filter, and an answer to what happens when an amended trade is then cancelled.

D2 is the largest piece of missing data

The P&L view is a bonus item in the brief, and the design implements it fully: aggregate P&L by symbol and by book, a blotter P&L strip, day P&L, and a positions grid split into realised and unrealised. Three of those columns cannot be computed from the trade table.

  • Unrealised P&L and MARK need a current price per symbol. There is no such field on Trade, no endpoint serving it, and trade_events carries only trade.created, trade.amended and trade.cancelled. The board's "marks 1s ago" implies a second live channel that does not exist yet.
  • Realised P&L needs a stated cost convention. It is derivable from trades alone, but only once FIFO or average-cost is chosen; the two give different numbers on the same data.
  • Net quantity, average price and net notional are genuinely derivable from the existing rows and need nothing new.

The cheapest honest route is a synthetic mark: seed a reference price per symbol, walk it with a small random step on a server interval, and broadcast it on a new mark.updated event. That keeps the P&L view truthful about where its numbers come from, and it is the moving data the design's animation depends on anyway.

Smaller items

  • D3, cancellation reason. The dialog marks it required. Either add a nullable cancel_reason column, or write the cancellation as a TradeAmendment row whose changes JSON holds the reason, which also fixes D5.
  • D4 and D5, the audit trail. Adding source and action to TradeAmendment, and writing a row on cancel as well as on amend, makes the audit board buildable as drawn and turns the model into a general trade-event log. That is a rename away from being clearer: TradeEvent rather than TradeAmendment.
  • D6, the two unbacked filters. "Trade date: Today" needs a date range on trade_query_schema. "Desk: LONDON" has no field at all: the books are EQUITIES_UK, EQUITIES_US and TECH_GROWTH, none of which is a desk. Either drop the chip or make it a display alias for a book filter.
  • Mobile create form. The desktop overlays collect all eight create fields; the mobile one collects six, omitting trader and trade timestamp. If those are meant to come from the session and the server, create_trade_schema should omit them too, and the desktop overlays should stop asking. What was checked, and what was not

  • Checked: all 23 *.dc.html artboards, by extracting every column header, form label, filter chip and status badge and comparing the set against the brief's sample JSON, trade_schema, trade_query_schema, trade_events and the Prisma models.
  • Confirmed present, not missing: the version-conflict path. The amend overlay has no version field, correctly, since it is an internal concurrency token, and the rejection state is specified on the shared board as "Amend rejected · version conflict". Anticipation.dc.html:260
  • Not checked: visual rendering. Every finding here comes from reading the artboard source, not from observing a browser. Arithmetic in the P&L and KPI figures was reconciled in an earlier pass and was not re-derived here.
